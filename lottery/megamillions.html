MEGA MILLIONS.

Five numbers. One Mega Ball. One massive jackpot.

HOW TO PLAY

Mega Millions is a lottery game where you purchase tickets and try to match the randomly drawn winning numbers.

NUMBER SELECTION

  • 5 white numbers from 1–70.
  • 1 gold Mega Ball from 1–24.
  • Each ticket costs $5.
  • Numbers are automatically selected for you.

MEGAPLIER

Every ticket receives a random multiplier: 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X, or 10X. It multiplies non-jackpot prizes.

WINNING

Match the winning numbers to earn a prize. Matching all five white balls and the gold Mega Ball wins the jackpot.

IMPORTANT

Jackpot odds are approximately 1 in 290,472,336. All credits and prizes in Null Casino are virtual.
