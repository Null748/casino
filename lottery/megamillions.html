HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS

Each ticket costs $5. Every ticket receives five white numbers from 1–70 and one gold Mega Ball from 1–24.

Purchase as many tickets as you want, then start the drawing. Your numbers are compared against the winning numbers.

MEGAPLIER

Every ticket receives a random multiplier: 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X, or 10X. It increases non-jackpot prizes.

THE JACKPOT

Match all five white numbers and the gold Mega Ball to win the jackpot. The chance of hitting the jackpot is approximately 1 in 290,472,336.

IMPORTANT

This is a simulated lottery game using virtual casino credits. No real money is involved. The odds are intentionally very low, but winning is possible.
