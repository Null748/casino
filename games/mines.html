HOW TO PLAY

Choose your bet and the number of mines, then start the round. Click tiles to reveal hidden gems. Every safe tile increases your multiplier. Cash out whenever you want!

TILE KEY

💎 GEM: A safe tile. Your multiplier increases.

💣 MINE: Hit one and lose your bet.

HIDDEN TILE: Unknown until selected.

IMPORTANT

• Choose between 1 and 24 mines before starting.
• More mines mean higher potential multipliers.
• Cash out to secure your current payout.
• If you hit a mine, the entire bet is lost.
