A growing collection of casino-style games and interactive experiences, all brought together in one place.

## 🎮 Available Games

### 🎲 Roulette
A classic roulette experience where players can place bets and test their luck against the wheel.

### ♠️ Blackjack
A classic single-player Blackjack game where players compete against the dealer to get as close to 21 as possible without going over.

- **Hit:** Draw another card.
- **Stand:** Keep your current hand and let the dealer play.
- **Double Down:** Double your bet and receive one final card.
- **Virtual Balance:** Start with $1,000 in virtual credits.
- **Blackjack Payouts:** Natural Blackjack pays 3:2.

### 🎰 Slots
A classic slot machine experience. Spin the reels, match symbols, and try your luck at landing a big payout.

### 🔴 Crash
A high-risk multiplier game where players must cash out before the multiplier crashes. The longer you wait, the bigger the potential payout.

### 🔺 Plinko
Drop a ball into the Plinko board and watch it bounce through the pegs before landing in a payout slot. Choose your risk level and test your luck.

### 🃏 Scratch Cards
Purchase virtual scratch cards and reveal hidden symbols. Match three or more symbols to earn a prize based on their rarity.

### 💣 Mines
A grid-based game where players uncover hidden tiles while avoiding mines. Each safe tile increases the potential multiplier, but one wrong move can cost the entire bet.

### 🎡 Wheel
Spin a multiplier wheel and see where it lands. Choose between two different wheel sizes, each with its own set of multipliers and risks.

### 🔢 RNGdle
A number-based RNG experience that continuously rolls random numbers. While not traditionally a casino game, RNGdle brings its own element of randomness and unpredictability.

- **Standard Mode:** Continuously generates random numbers.
- **Gambling Mode:** Introduces a virtual money system, allowing players to use their balance while participating in the RNG experience.

## 🎟️ Lottery

### 🔴 Powerball
A simulated lottery experience where players purchase number sets and enter a drawing for a chance to win a massive jackpot.

- **Ticket Cost:** $1 per number set.
- **Number Selection:** Five white numbers (1–69) and one red Powerball (1–26).
- **Winning Numbers:** Each drawing generates a new random set of winning numbers.
- **Prize Tiers:** Multiple prize levels based on how many numbers match.
- **Jackpot:** A growing simulated jackpot with extremely low, but mathematically possible, winning odds.
